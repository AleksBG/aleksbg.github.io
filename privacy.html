Privacy Policy

Applies to the application “Hermes Bot”. Last updated: 28 September 2026.

1. Who this policy covers

Hermes Bot is a private, single-user assistant operated by one individual for their own Google Account. It is not a public or multi-user service: no other person can create an account or sign in to it. This policy describes how Hermes Bot accesses, uses, stores and shares information obtained from Google APIs.

2. What Google data is accessed

After the account owner grants consent through Google's OAuth screen, Hermes Bot can access the following data in that owner's own account:

  • Google Calendar — events, calendars, availability, and event details.
  • Gmail — message contents, headers, labels and attachments; permission to modify labels and state, and to send mail on the owner's behalf.
  • Google Drive — files and folders the owner has stored there.
  • Google Docs and Google Sheets — document and spreadsheet contents.
  • Google Contacts — read-only access to the owner's contacts and their email addresses.
  • Basic account information — the email address of the account that granted access.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks that the account owner explicitly requests, for example:

  • creating, moving or removing calendar events and reporting availability;
  • triaging incoming email and preparing or sending replies, reminders and notifications the owner asks for;
  • reading, creating and updating documents and spreadsheets the owner designates;
  • resolving contact names to email addresses when the owner asks for mail to be sent.

Hermes Bot performs no other processing of Google user data.

4. Storage, security and retention

Hermes Bot runs on a computer controlled by the account owner. Google OAuth tokens are stored locally on that machine in a credentials file that is accessible only to the owner's own user account; they are never published, shared or committed to a public repository.

Content of emails, calendar events, documents, sheets and contacts is processed in memory to complete a request. Copies are written to the owner's local machine only when needed for the task at hand (for example, a generated report or a cached extract), and are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Data is retained only as long as it is required to provide the assistant's functions. The owner can delete locally stored copies at any time.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold, rented, traded or shared with third parties. It is not used for advertising, profiling or marketing, and it is not used to develop, improve or train generalised machine-learning models.

Data leaves the owner's machine only when a request requires it — for example, when the configured language model provider is used to interpret a command, or when a message is sent through Gmail. Transfers use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No data is transferred to third parties for any purpose other than performing the owner's own request.

6. Google Limited Use disclosure

Hermes Bot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Your choices and control

  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored token.
  • Deleting the local credentials file on the owner's machine removes all stored authorisation.
  • Requests to review, export or delete data held locally can be sent to the contact address below.

8. Children

Hermes Bot is not directed at children and is not available to the general public.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about data handled by Hermes Bot: onehermesbot@gmail.com.
